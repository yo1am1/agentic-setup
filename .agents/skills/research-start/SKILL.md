# research-start

Read [../../workflows/research.md](../../workflows/research.md) and [../../rules/guardrails.md](../../rules/guardrails.md).
Fix objective, one primary metric and direction, evidence, tunables, correctness
gates and budget. Do not invent a metric merely to justify a loop.
Measure a baseline. Isolate trials where edits could interfere with other work.
Change one hypothesis at a time; preserve negative results and failure evidence.
Keep strict improvements only if all gates pass. Stop after three non-improvements,
exhausted ideas, budget exhaustion or user stop unless the study is tighter.
Return real commands, baseline, champion, limitations and next hypotheses.
